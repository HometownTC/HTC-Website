*WINTER HAVEN · November 27, 2026*

A Florida short sale has no fixed timeline. It depends on who owns the loan and how quickly the servicer has what it needs. The published rules give checkpoints. On a Fannie Mae loan, the servicer answers a complete offer within 30 calendar days. On an FHA loan, the seller has four months from the Approval to Participate to get a contract. Plan around those checkpoints, not a promised closing date.

**At a glance**

- **What this is:** the timeline rules for Fannie Mae and FHA short sales, side by side.
- **Why it matters:** your contract's approval deadline and closing date have to fit the servicer's clock.
- **What the negotiator does:** completes the package and responds to the servicer's requests.
- **What HTC does:** tracks each checkpoint weekly and flags dates before they pass.
- **Your next step:** ask your negotiator which investor owns the loan, then map its checkpoints onto your contract dates.

## First, know which rules apply

Short sale timelines come from the loan's investor, not from Florida law. Three common groups:

- **Fannie Mae conventional loans** follow the Fannie Mae Servicing Guide.
- **FHA-insured loans** follow HUD Handbook 4000.1, Pre-Foreclosure Sale rules.
- **Freddie Mac, VA, portfolio and private loans** follow their own investor guides. Ask the servicer or negotiator for the governing timeline.

## Fannie Mae checkpoints

From [D2-3.3-01](https://servicing-guide.fanniemae.com/svc/d2-3.3-01/fannie-mae-short-sale), updated August 13, 2025:

| Step | Time allowed |
| --- | --- |
| Servicer acknowledges the offer | Within 5 business days |
| Servicer approves, counters or declines | Within 30 calendar days of a complete package (if one is required) and the offer |
| Borrower responds to a counteroffer | Within 5 business days |
| Servicer decides on a revised offer | Within 10 business days |
| Sale closes after approval | Within 60 calendar days, unless the servicer gets Fannie Mae's written OK to extend |

The valuation has its own clock. Under [F-1-14](https://servicing-guide.fanniemae.com/svc/f-1-14/processing-fannie-mae-short-sale), results are due within 10 calendar days of the servicer's request. The valuation must be dated or refreshed within 90 calendar days of approval.

## FHA checkpoints

FHA calls a short sale a Pre-Foreclosure Sale. These steps are in HUD Handbook 4000.1, section III.A.2.j.ii, as revised by [Mortgagee Letter 2025-12](https://www.hud.gov/sites/dfiles/OCHCO/documents/2025-12hsgml.pdf), effective October 1, 2025:

| Step | Time allowed |
| --- | --- |
| Servicer receives the signed Approval to Participate | Within 10 days of delivering it |
| Seller retains a listing broker | Within 7 days of delivery |
| MLS listing before offers are evaluated | At least 15 days |
| Seller gets a sales contract | Within 4 months of the Approval to Participate |
| Servicer sends the Sales Contract Review notice | Within 5 business days of receiving an executed contract |

The Sales Contract Review notice is not the final approval. Our [FHA 88%, 86%, 84% explainer](/resources/fha-short-sale-88-86-84-rule-florida/) walks through the approval steps that follow.

## Why incomplete files take longer

On Fannie Mae loans, the 30-day response window is measured from a complete package and offer. Until the servicer treats the package as complete, that window has not begun. Servicers handle missing or outdated documents differently. Some ask for one item; others ask for a fresh set. Ask the negotiator what the servicer has confirmed as received, and when.

Other things that add time: counteroffers, second mortgages and other liens, mortgage insurance review, and a loan transfer to a new servicer.

## A worked example

A listing agent signs a contract on a Fannie Mae loan on March 3. The negotiator submits it the same day. The servicer acknowledges it on March 6 and asks for one missing bank statement. The statement goes in March 10, and the servicer confirms the package is complete on March 12.

The 30-day response window now runs to about April 11. If the servicer approves April 9, the sale must close by about June 8, unless Fannie Mae approves an extension. Every date after March 12 hinges on that "complete" confirmation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does a short sale take in Florida?

There is no set length. Fannie Mae servicers answer a complete offer within 30 calendar days. FHA sellers have four months from the Approval to Participate to get a contract.

### When does Fannie Mae's 30-day clock start?

When the servicer has the offer and a complete borrower package, if one is required.

### How long do I have to close after approval?

On Fannie Mae loans, 60 calendar days unless an extension is approved. Always check the approval letter's expiration date.
