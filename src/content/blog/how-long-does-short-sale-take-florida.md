*WINTER HAVEN · December 8, 2026*

A Florida short sale has no fixed timeline. It depends on who owns the loan and how fast the seller's package is complete. The published rules give checkpoints. On a Fannie Mae loan, the servicer answers a complete offer within 30 calendar days. On an FHA loan, the seller has four months from approval to participate to get a contract. Plan the file around those checkpoints, not a promised closing date.

## The clock starts when the package is complete

Most short sale delays happen before the lender's clock even starts. Under [Fannie Mae's short sale rules](https://servicing-guide.fanniemae.com/svc/d2-3.3-01/fannie-mae-short-sale), the 30-day response window begins only after the servicer has the offer and a complete borrower package, when one is required.

A missing page, an old bank statement or an unsigned form can restart the wait. That is why weekly status checks matter. They catch the gap while there is still time to fix it.

## Fannie Mae checkpoints

These come from [D2-3.3-01](https://servicing-guide.fanniemae.com/svc/d2-3.3-01/fannie-mae-short-sale), updated August 13, 2025:

| Step | Time allowed |
| --- | --- |
| Servicer acknowledges the offer | Within 5 business days |
| Servicer answers: approve, counter or decline | Within 30 calendar days of a complete package and offer |
| Borrower responds to a counteroffer | Within 5 business days |
| Servicer decides on a revised offer | Within 10 business days |
| Sale closes after approval | Within 60 calendar days, unless the servicer gets Fannie Mae's written OK to extend |

The property valuation has its own clock. Under [F-1-14](https://servicing-guide.fanniemae.com/svc/f-1-14/processing-fannie-mae-short-sale), valuation results are due within 10 calendar days of the servicer's request. The valuation must be dated or refreshed within 90 calendar days of the approval.

## FHA checkpoints

FHA calls a short sale a Pre-Foreclosure Sale. Its rules are in HUD Handbook 4000.1, updated by [Mortgagee Letter 2025-06](https://archives.hud.gov/news/2025/2025-06hsgml.pdf), effective February 2, 2026:

| Step | Time allowed |
| --- | --- |
| Borrower returns the signed Approval to Participate | Within 10 days of delivery |
| Borrower retains a listing broker | Within 7 days of delivery |
| Listing in MLS before offers are evaluated | At least 15 days |
| Borrower gets a sales contract | Within 4 months of the Approval to Participate |
| Servicer reviews an executed contract | Within 5 business days |

FHA also lowers the minimum acceptable net proceeds as the marketing days add up. See our explainer on the [88%, 86% and 84% tiers](/resources/fha-short-sale-88-86-84-rule-florida/).

## What stretches the timeline

- **An incomplete package.** The servicer's clock does not start.
- **Counteroffers.** Each round adds days on both sides.
- **Second mortgages and other liens.** Each lienholder has to agree.
- **Mortgage insurance or investor review.** Another party may need to sign off.
- **Loan transfers.** A new servicer may need a new authorization and new documents.

Conventional loans not owned by Fannie Mae, VA loans and private loans follow their own investor rules. Ask the negotiator which rules apply before you build the timeline.

## What This Means for Your File

We build every short sale timeline around two dates: when the package went in complete, and when the approval letter expires. We check status weekly and log each answer, so a stalled file shows up in days, not months. This week, ask the negotiator on each pending short sale for the date the servicer confirmed the package was complete. If no one has that date, the 30-day clock may not have started yet.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does a short sale take in Florida?

There is no set length. Fannie Mae gives servicers 30 calendar days to answer a complete offer. FHA gives sellers four months to get a contract. Liens, counteroffers and missing documents add time.

### When does the lender's 30-day clock start?

On Fannie Mae loans, it starts when the servicer has the offer and a complete borrower package, if one is required.

### How long do I have to close after approval?

On Fannie Mae loans, the sale must close within 60 calendar days of approval unless the servicer gets written approval to extend. Always check the expiration date on the approval letter.
