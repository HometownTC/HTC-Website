*WINTER HAVEN · December 10, 2026*

On an FHA short sale, the servicer may approve an offer only if the seller's net proceeds meet a minimum share of the home's as-is appraised value. That share is 88% during days 1 to 30 of marketing, 86% during days 31 to 60, and 84% during days 61 to 120. The rule comes from HUD Handbook 4000.1, as updated by [Mortgagee Letter 2025-06](https://archives.hud.gov/news/2025/2025-06hsgml.pdf), effective February 2, 2026.

## FHA's name for a short sale

FHA calls a short sale a Pre-Foreclosure Sale, or PFS. The servicer, which HUD calls the mortgagee, runs the program. The seller first signs an Approval to Participate, or ATP. That is the borrower's written agreement to follow the program's rules.

Under [ML 2025-06](https://archives.hud.gov/news/2025/2025-06hsgml.pdf), the servicer must receive the signed ATP within 10 days of delivering it. The seller has seven days from delivery to hire a listing broker.

## The marketing clock

The seller has four months from the ATP to get a sales contract. The home must be listed in the MLS for at least 15 days before offers are evaluated.

Once an executed contract reaches the servicer, it has five business days to review it. That is the moment the tiers matter.

## The three tiers

| Days of marketing | Minimum net sale proceeds |
| --- | --- |
| 1 to 30 | 88% of as-is appraised value |
| 31 to 60 | 86% of as-is appraised value |
| 61 to 120 | 84% of as-is appraised value |

"Net sale proceeds" is not the price. It is what is left for the loan after the allowable closing costs come out. The servicer works out that figure from the settlement numbers.

"As-is appraised value" is the appraisal of the home in its current condition, not after repairs.

## A worked example

Say the as-is appraisal comes in at $300,000.

- **Days 1 to 30:** the net must be at least $264,000 (88%).
- **Days 31 to 60:** at least $258,000 (86%).
- **Days 61 to 120:** at least $252,000 (84%).

An offer that falls short in week two might work in week ten. That is why the listing date and the ATP date both belong on the file. The tier depends on how long the home has been marketed, so pricing and offer strategy change as the days add up. Those decisions stay with the listing agent and the seller.

## What the tiers do not cover

The tiers set the minimum the servicer may accept. They do not promise approval. The servicer still reviews the whole file, and second liens or other conditions can still hold up a deal.

FHA rules apply only to FHA-insured loans. Conventional, VA and private loans use their own investor rules. Confirm the loan type before you apply any of these numbers.

## What This Means for Your File

On an FHA short sale, we put three dates on the file the day it opens: the ATP delivery date, the MLS list date and the four-month contract deadline. We flag day 30, day 60 and day 120 so the listing agent sees each tier change before it happens. We track the dates; pricing and offer decisions stay with you and your seller. This week, pull the ATP on every FHA short sale you hold and calendar the day it turns 31 and 61.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the 88/86/84 rule for FHA short sales?

It sets the minimum net proceeds an FHA servicer may accept: 88% of as-is appraised value in days 1 to 30 of marketing, 86% in days 31 to 60, and 84% in days 61 to 120.

### Is net sale proceeds the same as the sale price?

No. It is what remains for the loan after allowable closing costs are deducted.

### How long does the seller have to find a buyer on an FHA short sale?

Four months from the Approval to Participate. The home must be in the MLS at least 15 days before offers are evaluated.
