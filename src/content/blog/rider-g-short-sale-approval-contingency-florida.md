*WINTER HAVEN · November 23, 2026*

Rider G is the Short Sale Approval Contingency in the [Florida Realtors/Florida Bar Comprehensive Rider](https://www.floridarealtors.org/tools-research/form-descriptions) (CR-7 Rev. 10/21). It makes the contract depend on the seller's lender approving the deal. While approval is pending, only two dates run from the Effective Date: the initial deposit and the Short Sale Approval Deadline. Every other date, including inspections, the loan application, loan approval and closing, starts when the buyer receives the approval.

## What Rider G does

The rider says the contract is "contingent upon" the seller's lender approving three things: the price, contract terms and settlement statement; a payoff below the balance owed; and the release of the mortgage. That approval is called "Short Sale Approval." It applies to all lienholders, not just the first mortgage.

An approval that leaves the seller exposed to a deficiency, or adds new terms for either party, does not count. It counts only if the affected party accepts it in writing. The seller must deliver an accepted approval to the buyer and closing agent within 3 days.

## What runs and what waits

Paragraph 5 is the one most agents miss.

| Runs from the Effective Date | Starts when the buyer receives Short Sale Approval |
| --- | --- |
| Initial deposit | Additional deposit |
| Short Sale Approval Deadline (90 days if left blank) | Inspection period |
| | Loan application and loan approval periods |
| | Title, survey and every other contract period |
| | Closing: 45 days after approval if left blank |

The parties can change this in the contract's additional terms, for example by setting an earlier inspection period. Unless they do, the dates in the right column wait for approval.

## Why you extend before the deadline

Paragraph 4 gives the seller until the Short Sale Approval Deadline to deliver approval. After that date, either party may cancel in writing and the buyer gets the deposit back.

Thirty days after the deadline, including any extensions, the contract ends automatically. No one has to send a notice.

**Example:** Effective Date January 5, blank left empty. The deadline is April 5. If approval has not arrived and no one extended, either side may walk away starting April 6. On May 5 the contract terminates on its own.

Most servicers take longer than 90 days. That is why the extension should be signed before the deadline passes, every time, with the new date written on the file.

## Spending money before approval

Paragraph 9(b) is direct. The seller and broker are not liable for the buyer's costs, "such as payments for loan applications, inspections and appraisals," if the lender does not complete the short sale.

Two other risks sit behind that line. The lender may deny the short sale. Or it may approve different terms, such as a higher price, and the buyer may not accept them. Either way, money spent on inspections and appraisals before approval may be lost.

That is why Rider G starts those periods after approval by default. When to inspect or apply for a loan is the buyer's decision, made with the buyer's agent.

## When the approved buyer cancels

An approval covers "terms of this Contract," meaning this buyer, this price and this settlement statement. A replacement buyer is a new contract with its own Rider G, Effective Date and approval deadline. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are inspections paused under Rider G?

Yes, by default. Only the initial deposit and the Short Sale Approval Deadline run from the Effective Date. Inspections, loan periods and closing start when the buyer receives approval, unless the contract says otherwise.

### What happens if the Short Sale Approval Deadline passes?

Either party may cancel in writing and the deposit goes back to the buyer. If approval still has not arrived 30 days after the deadline, the contract ends automatically.

### Does a short sale approval transfer to a new buyer?

Not automatically. The approval covers the approved contract, so a new buyer needs the servicer's review. [Part 2](/resources/approved-short-sale-buyer-cancels-fannie-freddie-fha/) covers each loan program's rules.
