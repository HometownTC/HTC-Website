*MIAMI · November 19, 2026*

Miami-Dade condo inventory is shrinking, not hitting a new high. Active condo listings fell 7.24% year over year to 11,706 in September 2026, the eighth straight monthly decline, according to [MIAMI REALTORS](https://www.miamirealtors.com/2026/10/16/miami-dade-breaks-annual-record-for-30-million-plus-home-sales-in-just-nine-months/). Supply still equals 12.3 months of sales. The association classifies that level as a buyer's market.

## Where Inventory Stands

Months' supply measures how long current listings would last at the current sales pace. Miami-Dade condos stood at 12.3 months in September. Single-family homes stood at 5.1 months, which the association calls a seller's market.

The count was higher a year ago. In September 2025, active condo listings rose 19.83% to 12,620, per [MIAMI REALTORS](https://www.miamirealtors.com/?p=142947). Supply then stood at 14 months. In November 2025, listings reached 12,733, per the association's [December 2025 report](https://www.miamirealtors.com/2025/12/19/miami-dade-older-condominiums-continue-selling-faster-than-newer-units-affordable-condo-sales-surge/).

## The Five-Year View

High is relative. September 2025 condo inventory was 16.7% below the September 2019 level of 15,142 listings. MIAMI REALTORS says the current total "is still significantly below pre-pandemic."

So the market has more listings than buyers can absorb quickly. It does not have more listings than it did before 2020.

## How Fast Condos Are Moving

Condo listings took a median 67 days to reach contract in September, down from 75 a year earlier. Median time to sale fell to 102 days from 115. Sellers received a median 94% of original list price.

Existing condo sales rose 1% to 909. The median price was $417,090, down 0.69%. Cash buyers made up 49.4% of existing condo sales.

In August, condo listings totaled 11,495, and supply stood at 12.1 months, per the association's [August report](https://www.miamirealtors.com/2026/09/16/miami-dade-on-pace-to-shatter-10m-up-home-sales-annual-record/).

## The Forces Behind Condo Supply

Florida's 2022 condo safety law created two building-level requirements. The first is the milestone inspection, a structural review of buildings three habitable stories or more, under [section 553.899](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/553.899). It is due by Dec. 31 of the year a building turns 30, then every 10 years.

The second is the structural integrity reserve study, or SIRS. It is a study of reserves for roofs, structure, plumbing and other major items, under [section 718.112](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/718.112). Most existing associations had a first-study deadline of Dec. 31, 2025. The statute sets Dec. 31, 2026 as the outside date.

Lending rules changed too. MIAMI REALTORS reports that Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac eliminated the limited review option for many condo loans on Aug. 3, 2026. Limited review was a shorter building approval path for some conventional loans. Fannie Mae's [Selling Guide announcement](https://singlefamily.fanniemae.com/media/document/pdf/announcement-sel-2026-07-selling-guide-updates) lists the limited review process as deleted.

Financing access remains narrow. Only 21 of 2,397 condo buildings in Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach are FHA-approved, per MIAMI REALTORS. The association says that "is preventing further market strengthening."

## What to Watch Next Quarter

Watch three numbers in the monthly MIAMI REALTORS report. First, whether listings keep falling for a ninth and tenth month. Second, whether months' supply drops below 12. Third, whether cash share holds near half, as financed buyers adjust to full building reviews.

## What This Means for Your File

In a low-inventory market, we prepare listings to move fast. In a high-supply market like Miami-Dade condos, we prepare listings to survive scrutiny. That means a complete disclosure package on Day 1. It means association documents ready before the first showing, including the milestone inspection and SIRS status. It means the estoppel ordered early. Our Miami-Dade listing checklist adjusts to current inventory. This Monday, pull every active condo listing. Confirm you have the association's milestone and SIRS documents on the file, and ask the lender-side contact whether the building needs a full review.
