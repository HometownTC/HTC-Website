*TALLAHASSEE · December 5, 2026*

First-time buyers made up 21% of U.S. home buyers in the National Association of REALTORS 2025 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers, the lowest share on record. The [NAR survey](https://www.nar.realtor/newsroom/first-time-home-buyer-share-falls-to-historic-low-of-21-median-age-rises-to-40) also found the typical first-time buyer is now 40 years old. First-time buyers are a smaller slice of the market, but each of their files still carries more steps.

## The National Number

NAR released the 2025 Profile on Nov. 4, 2025. It covers buyers who purchased a primary home between July 2024 and June 2025.

The first-time share fell to 21% from 24% in the prior survey. NAR calls it the lowest share since it began collecting the data in 1981, per its [April 2026 generational report](https://www.nar.realtor/newsroom/baby-boomers-remain-largest-share-of-home-buyers-as-first-time-buying-falls-to-record-low). Deputy Chief Economist Jessica Lautz said the share "has contracted by 50% since 2007."

The median age of first-time buyers reached 40, an all-time high. Repeat buyers had a median age of 62.

## The Florida Picture

Florida Realtors does not publish a monthly first-time buyer share. Its most recent [Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers in Florida](https://floridarealtors.org/tools-research/reports/florida-home-buyers-sellers-profile) is the 2023 edition, updated in January 2024. In that report, first-time buyers were 20% of Florida buyers, compared with 32% nationally at the time.

That figure is nearly three years old. Treat it as a baseline, not a current reading.

## How the Money Comes Together

The typical first-time buyer put down 10%, the highest level since 1989, per NAR. Personal savings was the top down payment source, cited by 59%. Another 22% cited gifts or loans from family and friends.

Each of those sources adds paperwork. A gift usually needs a gift letter, a signed statement that the money does not have to be repaid. Lenders often want bank records that trace the gift from donor to buyer.

## Florida Housing Programs

The [Florida Housing Finance Corporation](https://www.floridahousing.org/programs/homebuyer-overview-page), the state's housing finance agency, offers 30-year fixed-rate first mortgages for first-time buyers through participating lenders. Its down payment help includes:

- **Florida Assist:** up to $10,000 as a 0%, deferred second mortgage. It is not forgivable.
- **Florida Homeownership Loan Program (FL HLP):** $12,500 at 3%, repaid over 30 years.
- **HFA Preferred and HFA Advantage PLUS:** 3%, 4% or 5% of the loan amount, forgiven at 20% per year over five years.

Florida Housing says this help is not available as stand-alone assistance. It must pair with a Florida Housing first mortgage.

## Hometown Heroes Status

The [Hometown Heroes](https://www.floridahousing.org/programs/homebuyer-overview-page/hometown-heroes) program serves income-qualified, first-time buyers in eligible occupations. These include health care, school staff, first responders, child care and the military. Its 2026 terms allow up to 5% of the first mortgage amount, with a $10,000 minimum and a $35,000 maximum.

The 2025 round opened Aug. 18, 2025, with $50 million. Florida Housing reported on Feb. 27, 2026 that it had [committed all available funding](https://www.floridahousing.org/press/press-releases/releases/2026/2026/02/28/press-releases/florida-hometown-heroes-program-2025---making-homeownership-a-reality-for-florida-s-families) for that fiscal year. Average assistance was about $16,000 per loan.

The state's 2026-27 budget includes $50 million for the program, per [Florida Realtors](https://floridarealtors.org/news-media/news-articles/2026/06/fresh-state-budget-targets-housing-supply-assistance). Florida Housing's program page does not state whether 2026 funds remain. Confirm availability with a participating lender before writing it into an offer.

## What This Means for Your File

A first-time buyer file usually needs more coordination. More documents, more explaining, more check-ins. That is not a criticism. It is realistic capacity planning. Gift letters, assistance program approvals and lender conditions all add touch points. This Monday, flag every first-time buyer file in your pipeline. For each one, list the down payment source, any assistance program and the lender contact, and confirm that each program's approval step has a date on the file.
