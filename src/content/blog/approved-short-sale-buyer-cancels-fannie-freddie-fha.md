*WINTER HAVEN · November 24, 2026*

A short sale approval covers one contract: that buyer, that price and that settlement statement. If the approved buyer cancels, the next buyer needs the servicer's review. The approval does not carry over on its own. Which rules govern that review depends on who owns or insures the loan: Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac or FHA.

**At a glance**

- **What this is:** the rules each loan program sets when an approved short sale loses its buyer.
- **Why it matters:** approval letters expire, and a new buyer restarts parts of the clock.
- **What the agents and negotiator do:** find the next buyer, write the new contract and submit it to the servicer.
- **What HTC does:** documents the cancellation, rebuilds the timeline and tracks the new review weekly.
- **Your next step:** on every approved short sale, write down the approval letter's expiration date.

## Servicer vs. investor: who sets the rules

The servicer is the company the seller pays each month. It processes the short sale. But when Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac owns the loan, or FHA insures it, the servicer must follow that program's published rules. Those rules set the clocks below.

## The governing terms, side by side

| | Fannie Mae | Freddie Mac | FHA (HUD) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rulebook | [Servicing Guide D2-3.3-01](https://servicing-guide.fanniemae.com/svc/d2-3.3-01/fannie-mae-short-sale) | [Seller/Servicer Guide 9208.2](https://guide.freddiemac.com/app/guide/section/9208.2) and [9208.3](https://guide.freddiemac.com/app/servicing/section/9208.3) | [Handbook 4000.1, III.A.2.j.ii](https://www.hud.gov/sites/dfiles/OCHCO/documents/2025-12hsgml.pdf) |
| Offer acknowledged | 5 business days | 5 business days | Not stated |
| Decision on an offer | 30 calendar days from a complete package and offer | 30 days from the purchase offer | Sales Contract Review notice within 5 business days of an executed contract, (K)(2) |
| If the offer falls short | Revised offer decided in 10 business days | Servicer must include an acceptable counteroffer | Must meet the net proceeds tier for the marketing day, (K)(3)(b) |
| Close after approval | 60 calendar days unless Fannie Mae extends in writing | 60 days of approving the purchase offer | Final approval before closing, (L)(1) |
| Other clocks | Valuation dated within 90 days of approval ([F-1-14](https://servicing-guide.fanniemae.com/svc/f-1-14/processing-fannie-mae-short-sale)) | Approved request can be [cancelled when the buyer walks](https://help.sf.freddiemac.com/servicing-gateway/resolve/cancelled_status.htm) | Contract within 4 months of the Approval to Participate, (I)(1) |

Portfolio, private and VA loans follow their own rules. Ask the negotiator which rulebook applies before planning the next step.

## What the table means for a replacement buyer

- **Fannie Mae:** the new contract is a new offer to evaluate. The valuation must be dated or refreshed within 90 days of the new approval, so an older one may need updating.
- **Freddie Mac:** the approved request can move to Cancelled status. A new purchase offer falls under the same 30-day decision rule.
- **FHA:** the new executed contract gets its own Sales Contract Review. A later contract may qualify at a lower tier: 86% after day 30, 84% after day 60. But the four-month window from the Approval to Participate keeps running.

A buyer at the same price and net may move faster. Nothing in these rules requires the servicer to keep the old approval.

## Bringing in the next buyer: a checklist

1. Close out the first contract in writing: cancellation, deposit release and the reason.
2. Tell the negotiator the same day, so the servicer hears it from the seller's side first.
3. Ask the servicer how it wants the new buyer submitted: an amended approval or a new review.
4. Write the new contract with its own Rider G, Effective Date and Short Sale Approval Deadline.
5. Match the approved price and net where possible.
6. Note the old approval letter's expiration date, and ask for an extension if the new buyer can't close in time.
7. If the seller checked Rider G Paragraph 7(b), look at any back-up offers already accepted.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does a short sale approval transfer to a new buyer?

No. The approval covers the approved contract. A new buyer needs the servicer's review under the loan program's rules.

### How long do I have to close after short sale approval?

On Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac loans, 60 days from approval unless extended. On FHA loans, check the approval and final approval terms. Always confirm the date on the approval letter.

### Who sets short sale rules, the servicer or Fannie Mae?

The servicer processes the file, but it must follow the owner's or insurer's rules: Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac or FHA.
